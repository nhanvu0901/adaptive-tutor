# Inline Verification Fallback

Use this only when the separate `learn-verify` skill is unavailable. Turn the
claim into a falsifiable statement; search authoritative or primary sources;
compare the evidence to the claim; choose a verdict; then teach only the safe
form of that verdict.

- `confirmed`: evidence directly supports the claim.
- `qualified`: the core claim holds only with material conditions or caveats.
- `contradicted`: good evidence conflicts with the claim.
- `unknown`: trustworthy evidence is insufficient; do not teach it as fact.

State important conditions and uncertainty to the learner. Do not turn an
`unknown` result into a confident lesson claim.
